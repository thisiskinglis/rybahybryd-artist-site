Not entertainment. Evidence. Every track is a document. Every lyric, a scar mapped into sound. Three countries. Two decades. One man who refused to disappear.

Read My Story
My Story

Built from Scratch. More Than Once.

Poland — Childhood POLSKA

Block culture. Cassette tapes. Paktofonika at twelve years old, outside Wawel Castle with my father, before he was gone. That bench, that cassette, that evening — became the root of everything. Music wasn't a hobby after that. Grew up in Grajewo — running an underground shop, organising shows, absorbing raw Polish hip-hop energy.

England — Migration ENGLAND

Migration. Graft. Learning to survive in a language that wasn't mine. Bradford, London, Northwich — each city a different floor of the same building. England didn't welcome me — it tested me. Wormwood Scrubs. Crack. Robberies. When I saw the music industry up close I saw the lies. I stepped back. Not because I couldn't do it. Because I refused to do it wrong.

The Dark Years — 2021 FALLING DOWN

Addiction. Wrong choices. Consequences that weren't hypothetical. Locked in the house with my own poison. Drugs. Gambling. Back to crack. Then shooting meth. Complete loss. Death or prison was the only two doors left. This is the honest version. I'm not going to dress it up.

Ballito, South Africa — 2024 STANDING UP AGAIN

KJ gave me her hand. She took me away from both of those doors. I flew to South Africa with nothing in my name. November 2024 — reborn. No rehab. No system. Just radical honesty, KJ, Nerys, and the decision to face every shadow directly. I said sixteen years ago: I'll make music in my forties, when I have a story. This is the story.

HYBRYD Manifesto

This Is What We Stand For.

Who Is Ryba

Smok Ryba. Dragon Fish. In Polish folklore, the Smok breathes fire and refuses to die. The Ryba moves through every water — cold, dark, deep — and comes back up. I am both. Born in one country. Forged in another. Living in a third. I never fit the tank I was put in. That was never the point.

What Is HYBRID Music

Not a genre. A collision. Polish soul. UK rap. South African space. Three countries in one voice — no apology, no translation. Dark. Raw. Mystical. Real life reported, not performed. The music that happens when you stop trying to be one thing and realise the collision itself is the sound.

The Time

No date. No time. No matter. Start from tomorrow — but every day is yesterday. I said this sixteen years ago: 'I'll make music at my 40s when I have a story.' The music isn't late. It arrived exactly when it was supposed to. Everything that tried to stop it just became material.

The Mission

MentalMentorStudio® isn't a label. It's a signal. For the lost, raw, and real. The MMs philosophy was not written in a classroom. It was written in every room RYBA survived. If you have been to the bottom of the building, you know every floor. That is the qualification. The frequency is already running.
